# Action Verbs for CVs

Using strong action verbs at the start of bullet points makes your achievements more impactful and engaging for both ATS systems and human readers.

## Leadership & Management

| Verb | Use When... |
|------|-------------|
| Led | Directing teams or initiatives |
| Managed | Overseeing people, projects, or processes |
| Directed | Guiding strategic direction |
| Coordinated | Organizing multiple elements |
| Supervised | Overseeing direct reports |
| Mentored | Guiding professional development |
| Delegated | Assigning responsibilities |
| Facilitated | Enabling collaboration |
| Orchestrated | Managing complex initiatives |
| Spearheaded | Pioneering new initiatives |

**Examples:**
- Led cross-functional team of 12 engineers to deliver platform upgrade
- Managed $5M budget across 3 concurrent projects
- Mentored 5 junior developers through structured training program

## Tips for Using Action Verbs

1. **Start every bullet point** with an action verb
2. **Vary your verbs** - don't repeat the same verb
3. **Match verb to achievement** - choose the most accurate verb
4. **Keep tense consistent** - past tense for previous roles, present for current
5. **Avoid weak verbs** - replace "helped," "worked on," "was responsible for"

### Weak vs. Strong Examples

| Weak | Strong |
|------|--------|
| Was responsible for managing team | Led team of 8 engineers |
| Helped improve website performance | Optimized website reducing load time by 50% |
| Worked on customer issues | Resolved 500+ customer issues monthly |
| Assisted with project delivery | Delivered 12 projects on time and under budget |
