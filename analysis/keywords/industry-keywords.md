## How to Use These Keywords

1. **Match job posting**: Identify keywords from the job description that match your experience
2. **Natural integration**: Incorporate keywords naturally into your CV, not as a keyword dump
3. **Context matters**: Use keywords within achievement statements showing impact
4. **Avoid keyword stuffing**: ATS may flag excessive repetition
5. **Include variations**: Use both acronyms and full terms (e.g., "PMP (Project Management Professional)")
