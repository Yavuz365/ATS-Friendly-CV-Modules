# Education Module

## ATS-Friendly Format

```
EDUCATION

[Degree Type] in [Major/Field of Study]
[University/Institution Name] | [City, State/Country] | [Graduation Date]
[Optional: GPA (if 3.5+), Honors, Relevant Coursework]
```

## Guidelines

- List most recent education first
- Include graduation date (or expected graduation)
- GPA is optional; include if 3.5+ or if recently graduated
- Add relevant coursework for entry-level positions
- Include honors, awards, and scholarships
- Certifications can be in this section or separate

## Example

```
EDUCATION

Master of Science in Computer Science
Stanford University | Stanford, CA | May 2020
GPA: 3.8/4.0 | Specialization: Machine Learning

Bachelor of Science in Software Engineering
University of California, Berkeley | Berkeley, CA | May 2017
Magna Cum Laude | Dean's List (6 semesters)
Relevant Coursework: Data Structures, Algorithms, Database Systems, Software Architecture
```

## For Recent Graduates

Include more detail:
- Relevant coursework
- Academic projects
- Research experience
- Leadership roles in student organizations
- Study abroad programs

## For Experienced Professionals

Keep it brief:
- Degree, institution, and date
- Notable honors only
- Remove GPA after 3-5 years of experience

## ATS Tips

- Use standard section headers (Education, Academic Background)
- Spell out degree names (Bachelor of Science, not B.S.)
- Include full institution names
- Use consistent date formatting throughout CV
- Avoid abbreviations that ATS may not recognize
