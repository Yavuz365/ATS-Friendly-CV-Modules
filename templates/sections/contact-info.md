# Contact Information Module

## ATS-Friendly Format

```
[Full Name]
[City, State/Country] | [Phone Number] | [Email Address]
[LinkedIn URL] | [Portfolio/GitHub URL (if applicable)]
```

## Guidelines

- **Full Name**: Use your legal name or professional name consistently
- **Location**: City and state/country are sufficient; full address is optional
- **Phone**: Include country code for international applications
- **Email**: Use a professional email address (firstname.lastname@domain.com)
- **Links**: Include only relevant professional links

## Example

```
John Smith
San Francisco, CA | +1 (555) 123-4567 | john.smith@email.com
linkedin.com/in/johnsmith | github.com/johnsmith
```

## ATS Tips

- Avoid using tables or columns for contact information
- Place contact info at the top of your CV
- Use standard separators (pipe | or comma)
- Ensure email and phone are clickable in digital formats
- Do not include photos or personal information (age, marital status)
