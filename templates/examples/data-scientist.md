## Key Tips for Data Scientists

1. **Quantify model impact**: Accuracy, AUC-ROC, business metrics, revenue
2. **Show scale**: Data volumes, user counts, prediction throughput
3. **Include research**: Publications, Kaggle rankings, open source
4. **Balance technical and business**: ML techniques AND business outcomes
5. **Highlight MLOps**: Deployment, monitoring, A/B testing experience
