## Key Tips for Project Managers

1. **Quantify scope**: Budget size, team size, number of projects, duration
2. **Show methodology expertise**: Agile, Waterfall, SAFe, hybrid approaches
3. **Highlight stakeholder management**: Executive reporting, vendor negotiation
4. **Include certifications prominently**: PMP, CSM, SAFe are highly valued
5. **Demonstrate business impact**: Cost savings, efficiency gains, satisfaction scores
